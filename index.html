You can't

Frequently asked questions

How do I receive my template after buying?

Instantly. Checkout happens on Ko-fi, and your download is available right after payment — Ko-fi also emails you the link. If the email goes to spam, your files are always waiting in your Ko-fi purchase history.

What software do I need?

A free Google account covers the Google Sheets and Google Docs versions. Excel and Word files work in Microsoft Office and in free alternatives like LibreOffice. PDF forms can be filled in any modern PDF reader. No plugins or special software.

Can I use these for my clients?

Yes — for your own business operations, and you can send completed documents (like invoices you've filled in) to clients as normal. What you can't do is hand the blank template itself to clients or other businesses; that's redistribution.

Are these a subscription?

No. Every price here is one-time. The files are yours to keep and use for as long as you like. Bundle buyers also get updated bundle contents free when we improve them.

What if something doesn't work?

Message us through Ko-fi with a description (a screenshot helps). We fix genuine issues fast, and if we can't make it work as described, we refund you. See the honesty note above — that's a commitment, not fine print.

Can I get a refund if I change my mind?

Because these are instant-download digital files, we can't offer change-of-mind refunds — but if the product is broken or materially different from its description, you are covered by our guarantee above, no argument.

Do you take requests?

Gladly. Tell us on Ko-fi what template would save you time. The catalog grows based on what real freelancers and small businesses ask for.
